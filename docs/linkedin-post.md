## The post (copy everything between the lines)

---

We treat music as deeply personal — yet the second we hit play, it goes private. Spotify nailed playback; the social side leaked into group chats and screenshots you can't even press play on.

So I built **Circles** — a standalone app that adds the social layer to Spotify.

A few features, each solving a real problem:
→ **Public or private "circles"** — share with exactly the right people, not one fixed audience
→ **Reactions + replies** — songs you share finally get a response instead of going into a void
→ **Full in-app playback** — tap and the track plays instantly; no dead screenshots
→ **A daily auto-share agent** — autonomously posts your top track so your profile stays alive

North star: **weekly returning sharers** — people who come back AND post at least once. It captures the whole loop and ignores vanity signups. (Backed by activation, engagement, and D7/D30 retention.)

The kicker: I didn't hand-code this. I **vibe-coded the entire app with Claude** — I described the product and an AI agent planned it, wrote the full-stack code, debugged Spotify's API live off my screen, and built that autonomous share-agent into the product itself.

Real working app, not a mockup. Code's on GitHub 👇 Clone it and tell me what you'd add.

## Tips for the screen recording (high impact, ~25–30s)

Show the *loop*, not every screen:

1. **Log in** — "Connect with Spotify" → lands in the feed (proves it's real, not a mock).
2. **Play a track in-app** — tap a song, let it start, show the play/pause bar (the wow moment).
3. **Share + react** — open the composer, post a song to Public, then tap a reaction on a feed post.
4. **Search** — type an artist, show real Spotify song + artist results.

Recording tips:
- Resize the browser narrow (phone width) so it reads as a real mobile app.
- Keep it under 30 seconds; LinkedIn autoplays muted, so add 2–3 short text captions.
- End on the feed with a few shares visible so it looks alive.
- Export MP4 and attach it natively to the post (native video beats a link).

Tools: Windows **Xbox Game Bar** (Win+G) or the **Snipping Tool** screen-record.
